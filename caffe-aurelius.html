I
753 BC — The Seven Hills

Where Rome learned to gather

Every great city began with a table. In the Forum, merchants, poets and senators met to trade news before they traded goods. Caffè Aurelius carries that habit into the present: a room built for long conversations and short espressos.

We roast in small batches, bake every morning and keep one rule. No one is rushed.

III
110 AD — The Aqueducts

The craft of one cup

Select

We buy from three estates and taste every lot before it reaches the roaster.

Roast

Small batches, roasted twice a week, so each cup is never more than ten days old.

Brew

Water at 93°C, a 28-second pull, and a barista who watches every shot.

Serve

Poured at the table in warm ceramic, with a glass of cold water alongside.
