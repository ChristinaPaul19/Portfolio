Forge is a small, serious gym. Eight power racks, two platforms, a turf lane for sleds and a boxing corner, with no rows of mirrors and no queue for a barbell.

Every new member starts with a movement assessment and a written plan. Coaches are on the floor for every session, from the 5:30 am lifters to the late-evening regulars.
