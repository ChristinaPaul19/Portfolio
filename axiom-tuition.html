We show our working

Every student follows the same four steps. Scroll to see how Riya went from 45% to 86% in Maths.

Riya, Class 10 Maths

Start: 45%   Goal: 85%

1. Diagnose: 5 weak topics found

2. Plan: 8 weeks, 3 sessions a week

3. Practice: 40 problems every week

4. Review: weekly test and feedback

45% + understanding = 86% Well done!

1

Diagnose

A 60-minute assessment finds the exact topics that cost marks, not just the chapters the student says are hard.

2

Plan

The teacher writes an eight-week plan on one page. Parents get a copy and so does the student.

3

Practise

Short explanations, then problems, with a teacher checking every step. Mistakes are corrected on the spot.

4

Review

A weekly test and a five-line progress note. If a topic is still weak, it goes back into next week's plan.
